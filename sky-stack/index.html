MINING & BUILDING Blocks, miners, records

Tap exposed blocks to mine. Buried blocks must touch open space.

Upgrade your pick for permanent drag mining: Iron 2×2 costs 5 gold, Steel 3×3 costs 20, and Titanium 4×4 costs 50. Drag or hold after starting a stroke to keep swinging. Use MOVE to pan.

Miners prioritize blocks within reach, jump and climb toward work, and back away when stuck. They keep earlier mining abilities when upgraded: level 1 mines dirt, level 2 adds stone, and level 3 adds deepslate. Dirt hits on beats 1 and 3; stone on 2 and 4; deepslate on the “and” after 2 and 4.

Height records must stay stable for 3 seconds before they count.

MINER GHOSTS Hazards and revival

Miners drown after 5 seconds with their head underwater, or burn after a short stay in lava. Watch their air and heat bars.

Their ghosts sway upward with a spooky melody. Tap a ghost with any tool to revive the miner right there in a burst of pixels, keeping its level and pocket gold.

Revived miners have a brief protective shield. Ghosts wait above the world and save with your game. Gold claimed through the miner menu stays in your inventory.

INDUSTRY Ore deposits and smelting

Exposed terrain can show coal, iron, or copper flecks. Break it to collect both the block and its ore. Miners can mine deposits whenever they can mine the base material.

Open INDUSTRY to build a Furnace for 10 stone and 5 wood. Each ingot takes one ore, one coal, and four seconds. Auto-repeat waits for supplies; Pause holds the current batch.

Ore and ingots are stored resources, not tower blocks. Production pauses while you are away. Active Furnaces add a quiet musical pulse to the soundtrack.

TREES Wood, leaves, falling trunks

Wood takes two hits to chop. Every trunk block above the cut falls with gravity; you can mine the fallen logs too.

Unsupported leaves disappear in 1–2 seconds. Mine them first to collect leaf blocks.

Use WOOD and LEAVES in the toolbar to build. Placed leaf blocks stay put. On phones, swipe the toolbar to see every material.

LIQUIDS & SECRETS Water, lava, discoveries

Dirt floats in water. Stone and deepslate sink.

Water touching lava cools into solid obsidian.

No spoilers—valuable things can hide where you least expect them.
